Human-LLM Alignment — Human vs Model

This is NOT Cross-Model Agreement. It compares each selected model's predictions against your human annotations (Human_Action / Human_Consequence) — Lin's CCC (primary metric), plus MAE, RMSE, Pearson, and Spearman, computed independently for Action and Consequence valence. For "how similarly do models score each other," use Cross-Model Agreement instead.

Every selected model must already have a completed export for this prompt version.
